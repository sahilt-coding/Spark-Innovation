What We Offer

Our Programs

Two specialized tracks built for different age groups and learning goals.

🏗️ Ages 5–10

Elementary Engineering Camp

Hands-on build challenges — bridges, catapults, egg drops, and towers — with engineering concepts woven into every project. Kids learn to think like engineers: design, build, test, and improve.

💻 Ages 11–14

Middle School Coding

Introduction to coding — logic, problem-solving, and real programming fundamentals. Students work through challenges that build genuine computational thinking skills and a foundation for future development.
